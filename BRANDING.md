# Westminster name, forks and credit

Forks, submods, translations and experiments are welcome, including public releases.
Please credit the original project and link back to [Westminster](https://github.com/jealousZebra/ck3_westminster).

A short credit in your README and Workshop description is enough:

> Based on Westminster: UK Politics for Crusader Kings III, by the Westminster contributors.
> Original project: https://github.com/jealousZebra/ck3_westminster

Give your fork a distinct title and thumbnail, and make clear who maintains it. You may describe it as “based on
Westminster”; please avoid presenting an independent fork as an official Westminster release or implying endorsement.
Keep the original credits and add your own contributors. Tell players which version your fork is based on.

The code remains [MIT](LICENSE): retain its copyright and licence notice. Original assets and documentation use
[CC BY 4.0](LICENSE-ASSETS.md): keep attribution, link the licence and indicate changes when reusing them.
AI-generated images and third-party material have separate terms listed in [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).
These branding requests do not add restrictions to those licences.
