## Crusader Kings III (Paradox Interactive)

Westminster is a modification of *Crusader Kings III*. Some files in the mod folders are copies of the game's own files
with changes (interface layouts, council positions, text formats, event backgrounds, portrait modifiers). They are
made by the build scripts from the installed game and say so in their first line. They remain the property of
Paradox Interactive AB and are included under Paradox's user-mod terms, only so that the mods work. You need your
own copy of the game to use them. The screenshots in `westminster/workshop/screenshots/` show the game.

*Crusader Kings* and *Paradox Interactive* are trademarks of Paradox Interactive AB. These mods aren't made, endorsed
or supported by Paradox.

## Real people

Westminster uses the real names of members of the 2024 Parliament, other public figures and the royal family. All
events are generic and fictional. Nothing in the mod says or implies anything about any real person. Some birth
dates are approximate. Spouses and family of MPs are invented. The mod isn't affiliated with Parliament, any
political party or the Royal Household. Party emblems are simplified designs inspired by the parties' symbols, not
their logos.
