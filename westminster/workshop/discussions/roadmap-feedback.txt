TITLE: Roadmap & Feature Requests

[h1]Help shape Westminster[/h1]

Westminster is growing from its 1979 and 2024 starts into a broader modern British political simulation. Suggestions are welcome, especially when they describe the political story or decision a feature would create for the player.

[h2]Current priorities[/h2]
[list]
[*]More historical starting dates and political figures
[*]Deeper elections, campaigning and parliamentary strategy
[*]More complete careers for backbenchers, party leaders and activists
[*]Additional modern interface and character presentation
[*]Balance, stability and clearer onboarding
[/list]

[h2]When suggesting a feature[/h2]
[list]
[*]Describe who would use it.
[*]Explain the choice or trade-off it creates.
[*]Mention any historical example that would help model it.
[*]Keep separate ideas in separate replies so other players can discuss them clearly.
[/list]

The roadmap may change as testing reveals what the game needs most, but thoughtful suggestions are read and considered.
