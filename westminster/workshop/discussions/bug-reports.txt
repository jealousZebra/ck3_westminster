TITLE: Bug Reports & Known Issues

[h1]Report a problem with Westminster[/h1]

Thank you for helping improve the mod. Please check the known issues below, then post one reply per problem so it is easy to track.

[h2]Include these details[/h2]
[list]
[*]What you were doing when the problem happened
[*]Which start date and character you were playing
[*]Whether you started a new game after the latest update
[*]Your CK3 version and enabled DLC
[*]Any other enabled mods
[*]A screenshot or save file, if useful
[/list]

[h2]Known beta limitations[/h2]
[list]
[*]Long campaigns may still need balance adjustments.
[*]Some less-used screens may retain medieval presentation.
[*]Roads to Power is required to play an unlanded activist or campaigner.
[*]Major updates require a new campaign unless the change notes say otherwise.
[/list]

Please keep reports factual and reproducible. Political debate belongs elsewhere; this topic is for gameplay and technical problems.
