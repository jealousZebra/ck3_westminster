TITLE: How to Play: Elections, Parliament & Becoming Prime Minister

[h1]Choose your route to Downing Street[/h1]

[b]Prime Minister[/b]
[list]
[*]Appoint a capable Cabinet and keep your party wings satisfied.
[*]Introduce bills, manage the whip and protect your working majority.
[*]Watch public trust and polling before calling an early election.
[/list]

[b]Party leader or backbench MP[/b]
[list]
[*]Build profile and political capital through parliamentary and party activity.
[*]Support or rebel against the whip, seek promotion and prepare for leadership contests.
[*]Use the State of the Parties button on the left of the screen to follow polls and the Commons.
[/list]

[b]Activist or campaigner[/b]
[list]
[*]Roads to Power is required for unlanded play.
[*]Complete political work to build Party Standing, reach the approved list and contest a selection.
[*]Win a constituency at a general election or by-election, or build support and found a new party.
[/list]

[h2]Winning elections[/h2]
Polls move monthly. Campaign choices, public trust, party leadership and political events affect the national picture, while every constituency retains its own starting lean. A strong national campaign still needs the right candidates in the right seats.

[h2]Forming a government[/h2]
The largest party may govern alone, negotiate a coalition or seek confidence and supply. A government that loses its Commons majority can face a confidence vote and an early election.
