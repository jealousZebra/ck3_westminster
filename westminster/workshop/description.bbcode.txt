[h1]Westminster[/h1]
Run Britain—or build a movement and fight your way to Downing Street. A modern UK politics total conversion for Crusader Kings III, with 650 constituencies and six playable starts.

[h2]Six starting dates[/h2]
[list]
[*]1945 — Clement Attlee: rebuild Britain after the war.
[*]1979 — Margaret Thatcher: take office after the election.
[*]1997 — Tony Blair: turn a landslide into reform.
[*]2010 — David Cameron: hold the coalition together.
[*]2016 — Theresa May: negotiate Brexit and win a Commons majority for a settlement.
[*]2024 — Keir Starmer: lead a new government.
[/list]

[h2]Build Your Movement[/h2]
Recruit members and donors, build local branches, recruit candidates and invite MPs to defect. Founded parties now compete in elections and appear in polls and seat counts. Organize locally to build support.

[h2]Govern, negotiate and campaign[/h2]
[list]
[*]Career actions: constituency surgeries, local projects, a public-service caucus and committee inquiries.
[*]Government: fiscal room, debt, public services, economic confidence and ministerial delivery plans.
[*]Brexit: choose a strategy, negotiate, put a settlement before Parliament and manage the transition.
[*]Press: fictional editorial relationships, interviews, endorsements and leaked-draft events.
[/list]

[h2]Start a fresh campaign[/h2]
For CK3 1.20. Start a new campaign with Westminster as the only enabled mod. Roads to Power is required for landless play. This update cannot be added to an existing campaign.

[h2]Beta notes[/h2]
Historical Parliaments use the fixed modern 650-seat map; some seat assignments and minor characters’ birth dates are approximate. Likenesses use CK3 DNA. Some medieval clothing remains. The four new starts passed opening checks on macOS; long campaigns, Linux and Windows validation of the new content remain outstanding. New in-game text may use English fallback in other languages.

[h2]Languages[/h2]
English, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Russian, Simplified Chinese and Spanish.
