[h1]Westminster: run Britain—or fight your way to Downing Street[/h1]
[b]A modern UK politics total conversion for Crusader Kings III.[/b]

Lead [b]Margaret Thatcher in 1979[/b], govern as [b]Keir Starmer after the 2024 election[/b], play any MP, or begin as an activist and build a new political party.

Fight elections across all 650 constituencies, manage Parliament and Cabinet, survive rebellions and confidence votes, shape public opinion and try to become Prime Minister.

[h2]Beta 0.5.4 — Windows Startup Fix[/h2]
Fixed a reproduced Windows crash before the main menu and made startup protections work with older launcher settings. Fresh 1979 and 2024 campaigns passed on Windows with both current and outdated launcher metadata; the combined build also passed macOS startup testing on CK3 1.20.1.1. Linux and long campaigns remain unverified.

[b]Please start a fresh campaign when testing this update, with Westminster as the only enabled mod.[/b]

[h2]1979 and 2024 starts[/h2]
[list]
[*]Two playable starts: Margaret Thatcher on 4 May 1979 and Keir Starmer on 5 July 2024.
[*]The 1979 Commons mapped across the fixed 650-seat game map.
[*]Historical party leaders, the first Thatcher Cabinet, Elizabeth II and other significant figures.
[*]Recognisable CK3 character models for leading politicians.
[*]A redesigned dark newspaper interface, modern political artwork and nine supported languages.
[/list]

[h2]Choose your path[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Prime Minister:[/b] appoint a Cabinet, control the parliamentary timetable, survive confidence votes and keep your party together.
[*][b]Party leader:[/b] set policy, manage internal factions and fight for Number 10.
[*][b]Backbench MP:[/b] build influence, rebel against the whip, seek promotion or challenge the leadership.
[*][b]Independent and activist:[/b] build support, create a party and contest elections. Roads to Power is required for unlanded play.
[/list]

[h2]Modern political Britain[/h2]
[list]
[*]All 650 Westminster constituencies represented on the map.
[*]General elections, by-elections, party leadership contests and confidence votes.
[*]Government, opposition, Cabinet, shadow Cabinet and parliamentary careers.
[*]Public opinion, political capital, party discipline and constituency support.
[*]Modern parties, ideologies, traits, lifestyles, decisions and events.
[*]A contemporary newspaper-inspired interface built for dark mode.
[/list]

[h2]Languages[/h2]
English, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Russian, Simplified Chinese and Spanish.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Built for [b]Crusader Kings III 1.20[/b]. Start a new game from the 1979 or 2024 bookmark; the mod cannot be added to an existing save.

[h2]Beta notes[/h2]
[list]
Westminster is actively developed. Long campaigns may still need balance work, some characters retain CK3-era clothing, and a few less-used screens still contain medieval presentation. The 1979 Commons is mapped onto the modern 650-seat constituency layout, using the closest appropriate seat where boundaries differ.
[/list]
