# rank ("Countess"), which here would read "MP Jane Smith" for an MP's wife. The holder entries (only_holder,
# priority 9600) keep the rank names of localization/english/replace/wm_ranks_l_english.yml; the spouse entries
# below them (priority 9500) give the spouse no rank. Feudal government only: the Crown's "Queen" is untouched.
