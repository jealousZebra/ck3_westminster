Westminster: credits and licences
==================================

Generated by westminster/tools/data/build_credits.py - edit there.

Westminster is a mod for Crusader Kings III. Its code is under the MIT licence and its original assets under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0), credit "the Westminster contributors". The material below keeps its
own terms.


Real people
-----------

Real members of the 2024 Parliament, other public figures and the royal family appear under their real names.
All events are generic and fictional. Nothing in the mod says or implies anything about any real person. Some
birth dates are approximate, and MPs' spouses and families are invented. The mod is not affiliated with
Parliament, any political party or the Royal Household. Party emblems are simplified designs inspired by the
parties' symbols, not their logos.


Crusader Kings III
------------------

Some files in this mod are copies of the game's own files with changes; each says so in its first line. They
remain the property of Paradox Interactive AB and are included under Paradox's user-mod terms. Crusader Kings
and Paradox Interactive are trademarks of Paradox Interactive AB. This mod is not made, endorsed or supported by
Paradox.
